HUMANS VS AI - PIXEL BEAM ASSET PACK
AI-READY IMPLEMENTATION GUIDE
=====================================

PURPOSE
-------
This file is the implementation contract for the animated Humans-vs-AI pixel beam asset pack.

It is written so that a coding AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Kimi, etc.) or a human developer can read it and implement the effect without needing extra visual interpretation.

CORE IDEA
---------
- ORANGE = Humans.
- CYAN/BLUE = AI.
- Humans push from LEFT -> RIGHT.
- AI pushes from RIGHT -> LEFT.
- The bright white impact/collision moves toward the LOSING side.
- If Humans are winning, the impact moves RIGHT (toward AI).
- If AI is winning, the impact moves LEFT (toward Humans).
- At 50/50, the impact is centered.
- The artwork is pixel art. Never blur or smooth it.

IMPORTANT:
The FULL collision animation is only a ready-made neutral/fixed clash.
For a percentage-driven beam, use the THREE MODULAR spritesheets:
1. beam-human-spritesheet.png
2. beam-ai-spritesheet.png
3. beam-impact-spritesheet.png

Tiled can store and animate the sprite frames, but the actual Human/AI percentage calculation and movement of the collision point should be controlled by game code.

============================================================
4. AUTHORITATIVE SCORE INPUT
============================================================

Required inputs:
- humanWins
- aiWins

Definitions:
- Human wins = students counted by the game's Human-side rule.
- AI wins = students counted by the game's AI-side rule.

IMPORTANT THRESHOLD NOTE:
The asset pack does not define what happens when a student's score is EXACTLY equal to the threshold.
Use the game's authoritative scoring rule.
Do not silently invent a new rule.

total = humanWins + aiWins

If total > 0:
    humanRatio = humanWins / total
    aiRatio = aiWins / total

If total == 0:
    humanRatio = 0.5
    aiRatio = 0.5

humanPercent = humanRatio * 100
aiPercent = aiRatio * 100

advantage = humanRatio - aiRatio

Range:
- advantage = -1.0 means complete AI domination.
- advantage =  0.0 means 50/50.
- advantage = +1.0 means complete Human domination.


============================================================
5. EXACT COLLISION POSITION FORMULA
============================================================

The modular artwork is designed around a 320 x 96 native beam area.

beamAreaWidth  = 320
beamAreaHeight = 96
centerX = 160
centerY = 48
leftVisibleBound  = 10
rightVisibleBound = 310
maxShift = 40

targetImpactX = clamp(160 + advantage * 40, 120, 200)
impactY = 48

Examples:

Human / AI    Expected impactX
--------------------------------
0 / 100       120
10 / 90       128
25 / 75       140
40 / 60       152
50 / 50       160
60 / 40       168
75 / 25       180
90 / 10       192
100 / 0       200

Direction rule:
- Human advantage -> impact moves RIGHT.
- AI advantage -> impact moves LEFT.
- Tie -> impact stays centered.

Why maxShift = 40?
The current side sprites are 200 px wide, with contact anchors at:
- Human x = 190
- AI x = 10

Keeping the collision between x=120 and x=200 gives a clear push effect without requiring horizontal distortion of the pixel art.

============================================================

Everything shares the same vertical center:
centerY = 48

IMPACT

humanSpriteX = -30

DRAW ORDER:

2. AI beam

4. Impact

The impact must be drawn last/on top so it hides the seam where the two beam sprites meet.


============================================================
7. DO NOT STRETCH THE PIXEL ART
============================================================

Do NOT horizontally scale the Human or AI sprite according to percentage.

Preferred:
- Keep every frame at its original proportions.
- Use CLIPPING / REVEALING.
- Move the collision point.
- Keep the artwork crisp.

Recommended native clip areas:

Human clip:
x from 10 to impactX

AI clip:
x from impactX to 310

Human drawing:
x = impactX - 190
y = 16

AI drawing:
x = impactX - 10
y = 16

If the renderer uses a different API, reproduce the same behavior using a scissor rectangle, mask, crop rectangle, clip-path, canvas clip, shader, or viewport system.

srcY = 0
srcW = 72

The three modular loops may run independently.
They do not need to restart together.


============================================================
9. SMOOTH MOVEMENT WHEN PERCENTAGES CHANGE
============================================================

Do NOT teleport the collision instantly when live percentages change.

Recommended transition:
400-800 ms

Good default:
600 ms

Use a controlled ease-in-out or ease-out.
Do not use spring/bouncy motion.

Concept:

oldImpactX = currentImpactX
newImpactX = calculateImpactX(newHumanRatio, newAiRatio)

Over about 600 ms:
currentImpactX = interpolate(oldImpactX, newImpactX, easedProgress)

The Human beam, AI beam, clipping regions, impact sprite, and extra sparks must all use currentImpactX while the transition is happening.

10. PERCENTAGE-DEPENDENT SPARKS
============================================================

The impact spritesheet already contains nearby animated sparks.

For a clearer "who is winning" effect, optionally generate EXTRA procedural pixel sparks in game code.

Do not replace the impact artwork.
These are additional sparks.

Human colors:

#F05A00
#FF8C00
#FFC928
#FFF0A0

AI colors:
#004A9F
#0079E8
#00C4FF
#62ECFF
#DFFFFF

Neutral:
#FFFFFF

Use hard pixel rectangles only.

Good native sizes:
1 x 1
2 x 2
3 x 3
4 x 2
2 x 4

No circles.
No border-radius.
No blur.

Recommended spark-rate mapping:

humanSparkRate = 4 + 16 * humanRatio
aiSparkRate    = 4 + 16 * aiRatio

Examples:

50/50:
Human = 12
AI = 12

75/25:
Human = 16
AI = 8

90/10:
Human ≈ 18
AI ≈ 6

100/0:
Human = 20
AI = 4

0/100:
Human = 4
AI = 20

The losing side should still produce some sparks.

Spawn near:
(impactX, impactY)

Human/orange sparks travel mostly:
- left
- upper-left
- lower-left

AI/cyan sparks travel mostly:
- right
- upper-right
- lower-right

White sparks may travel either direction very close to the impact.

Recommended lifetime:
150-350 ms.

Keep travel short and punchy.
Do not fill the whole screen with particles.


============================================================
11. PIXEL-PERFECT RENDERING RULES
============================================================

Mandatory:
- Use nearest-neighbor / pixelated rendering.
- Disable texture smoothing.
- Use integer coordinates where practical.
- Prefer integer display scaling: 2x, 3x, 4x, etc.
- Never resize source PNGs with bilinear filtering.
- Never add blur.
- Never add soft glow that destroys pixel edges.

Browser/CSS:
image-rendering: pixelated;

Canvas:
ctx.imageSmoothingEnabled = false;

Other engines:
Use nearest/point texture filtering.

13. GENERIC IMPLEMENTATION PSEUDOCODE
============================================================

STATE:
humanWins
aiWins
humanRatio = 0.5
aiRatio = 0.5
currentImpactX = 160
targetImpactX = 160
centerY = 48

SET SCORE:

function setScore(humanWins, aiWins):
    total = humanWins + aiWins

    if total <= 0:
        humanRatio = 0.5
        aiRatio = 0.5
    else:
        humanRatio = humanWins / total
        aiRatio = aiWins / total

    advantage = humanRatio - aiRatio

    targetImpactX = clamp(
        160 + advantage * 40,
        120,
        200
    )

    smoothlyMoveImpactTo(targetImpactX, 600ms)


EACH RENDER FRAME:

humanFrame = floor(timeMs / 100) % 4
aiFrame = floor(timeMs / 100) % 4
impactFrame = floor(timeMs / 100) % 6

impactX = currentImpactX
impactY = 48

humanX = impactX - 190
humanY = impactY - 32

aiX = impactX - 10
aiY = impactY - 32

impactXDraw = impactX - 36
impactYDraw = impactY - 36

1. Clip to Human region [10 .. impactX]
2. Draw current Human frame at (humanX, humanY)
3. End Human clip

4. Clip to AI region [impactX .. 310]
5. Draw current AI frame at (aiX, aiY)
6. End AI clip

7. Spawn/update optional percentage-weighted sparks

8. Draw current Impact frame at:
   (impactXDraw, impactYDraw)


EXPECTED RESULT:

Humans winning:
impactX > 160
collision moves right
orange side pushes farther
orange sparks occur more often

AI winning:
impactX < 160
collision moves left
blue side pushes farther
blue sparks occur more often

Tie:
impactX = 160
both sides balanced


============================================================
14. LIVE LEADERBOARD UPDATE BEHAVIOR
============================================================

Recommended sequence when new scores arrive:

1. Recalculate humanWins and aiWins.
2. Recalculate humanRatio and aiRatio.
3. Calculate targetImpactX.
4. Move currentImpactX toward targetImpactX over about 600 ms.
5. Keep all sprite animations playing continuously.
6. Increase extra spark activity on the winning side.
7. Optionally emit one short winning-color spark burst when the update happens.
8. Settle into the normal looping clash.

This should feel like one side physically pushed the other.


============================================================
15. TV / LARGE DISPLAY RECOMMENDATION
============================================================

Render the source effect at native 320 x 96 internally and scale the assembled result with nearest-neighbor.

Examples:
2x -> 640 x 192
3x -> 960 x 288
4x -> 1280 x 384

Choose the largest integer scale that fits the leaderboard layout.

Do not redraw or smooth-resize the source artwork.


============================================================
16. COMMON IMPLEMENTATION MISTAKES
============================================================

DO NOT:
- Import a spritesheet and assume Tiled knows the animation timing automatically.
- Use the GIF for the final dynamic score-driven version.
- Use the full baked collision spritesheet for percentage movement.
- Stretch the Human beam based on score.
- Stretch the AI beam based on score.
- Blur or smooth the pixel art.
- Make the collision jump instantly.
- Move the collision toward the WINNING side. It must move toward the LOSING side.
- Hide the losing beam entirely.
- Draw the impact underneath the side beams.
- Forget to clip/mask the side beams.
- Invent a new scoring source instead of using the game's authoritative one.
- Invent a threshold-equality rule if the project has not defined one.
